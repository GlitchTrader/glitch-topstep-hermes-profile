# topstep-position-management

Manage open positions actively; `HOLD` is not the default.

When positioned, write the compact `POSITION_MANAGEMENT_V1` ledger in `decision_audit.decisive_evidence` and replace every placeholder. `SELECTION_ACTION` must equal `action`. Put `prior_hypothesis` continuity in `disconfirming_evidence` when `recent_frames` is non-empty.

Gateway `AUTO_BREAKEVEN` and daily-capture protection are tighten-only and intent-free — never confuse them with Hermes `MOVE_STOP` structural amendments inside the original risk envelope.

For each open book reconstruct entry setup, current setup, native protection, peak favorable excursion, rollback, and remaining objective. After material favorable excursion or rollback, explicitly compare `HOLD`, `MOVE_STOP`, `MOVE_TP`, `EXIT`, and any independently justified protected addition. Rebase on current price and remaining objective — an unbroken original invalidation alone does not make `HOLD` superior.

Judge excursion in risk units and structure, not fixed dollar landmarks. Prefer supported protection or `EXIT` when remaining capture distance is available but continuation no longer compensates for giveback.
